Q4 data migration plan

Move billing and ledger tables off the legacy Postgres 11 cluster by 12 December, without customer-visible downtime.

MRMaya Reyes, with Claude CodeUpdated 2h ago

Why now

The pg-legacy-01 cluster reaches end of support in January, and the ledger tables now account for 71% of its write load. Every quarter we wait adds roughly 400 GB to the copy and another week of dual-write risk.

We would rather spend two careful weeks on the cutover than one frantic weekend on a rollback.Priya Natarajan, platform review, 3 October

Phases

PhaseWindowOwnerStatus
Schema parity & shadow tablesOct 6 – Oct 24Jonah T.Done

Backfill

We replicate with a publication per table group so the ledger can lag without holding back invoices. Batches stay under 50k rows to keep replica lag below two seconds.

-- on pg-legacy-01
create publication billing_pub
  for table invoices, invoice_lines, payments;

-- on pg16-billing
create subscription billing_sub
  connection 'host=pg-legacy-01 dbname=billing'
  publication billing_pub
  with (copy_data = true, streaming = parallel);

Risks we are watching

  • Sequences do not replicate; we reset them at cutover from max(id) + 10000.
  • The nightly invoice job holds long transactions that can stall the WAL sender.
  • Two reporting dashboards still query the legacy host directly.

Cutover checklist

  • Freeze schema changes on billing from 1 December
  • Rehearse cutover on staging with production-sized snapshot
  • Row-count and checksum reconciliation passes three nights running
  • Point reporting dashboards at the new read replica
  • Announce the 20-minute write pause to support and finance
