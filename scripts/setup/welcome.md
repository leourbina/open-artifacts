# Welcome to Open Artifacts

Your instance is up and running. This page was published by the setup
self-test, which means your agent connection works end to end.

## What you can do

- **Publish from any MCP-capable agent.** Ask it to publish a page, a
  document, a chart or a small app, and it lands here with a link.
- **Share with your team.** Keep artifacts private, share them with
  specific people, or set them to `team`, which makes them visible to all
  members.
- **Iterate.** Every publish to the same artifact creates a new version,
  and you can restore any earlier one.

## Next steps

1. Add the MCP server to your agent with the snippet setup printed.
2. Ask your agent to publish something.
3. Invite your team from the dashboard.

Everything runs on your own Cloudflare account. Nothing is sent anywhere
else.
